# Practical PBS consultation guidance — construction status

Actual actor: Codex in Corporate Chad conversation, October 17, 2026. No deployed officer or independent review is claimed.

Adds a rule-based consultation module to the existing workspace. It reuses the existing PBRS parser/recalculation and does not change the developmental scoring method. Customer imports cannot override readiness by editing exported score/status fields. Resource limits and observed results affect the guidance; no external model call, supplier recommendation commission or implied paid entitlement is introduced.

Guidance distinguishes declared practice from a working cause hypothesis. It asks about unresolved urgent risks, contradicting answers and missing records. Reported urgent risk precedes discretionary growth work; known Red areas precede unknown/discovery areas, then other assessed areas. Unknown practices are not scored as zero. The focus remains a starting rule, not a validated causal diagnosis or independently proven optimal priority.

Prelaunch, operating and expanding businesses receive appropriate stage context without a mandatory beginner retake. Zero available time leads to capacity resolution; limited time constrains the initial weekly work estimate and defers parallel initiatives. The budget input is customer context, not spending authorization. The templates require no supplier spend. They do not imply the eventual paid PBS service is free.

Work outlines cover decision briefs, customer experiments, cash visibility, delivery procedures, accountability/coverage, baseline learning logs, controls and continuity scenarios. Customers can draft field responses and download guidance plus their work. These new worksheet responses are page-memory only and are not silently added to earlier cloud draft records. The UI explicitly states this. Template fields are not reviewed work and no completion credit is generated.

Four proposed monthly tasks establish evidence, produce a usable draft, compare actual observations and choose the next consultation. These are guidance outlines, not registered server milestones until separately adopted through the execution interface. Worsening results pause expansion and trigger cause/side-effect investigation. Unchanged results prompt reconsidering the intervention and hypothesis rather than repeating tasks blindly. Improvement still requires checking other influences. Unknown effects remain unknown. Funding milestones do not terminate consultation or require more borrowing.

The cash worksheet calculates a customer-input cash-timing scenario. The unit-economics worksheet calculates contribution and simplified break-even units; non-positive contribution returns no defined break-even volume. Neither verifies financial condition or provides a legal, accounting or lending determination. Pricing inputs concern the customer's own hypothetical product, not approved PBS prices.

36 engine assertions and six synthetic-DOM assertions cover resource limits, urgent risk/tension, unknown coverage, forged score rejection, stage context, changed-result guidance, no-purchase guidance, post-funding continuation, arithmetic, invalid inputs, editable drafts and text safety. Full source suite totals 137 assertions. These are not real-browser, independent practitioner or comparative market validation.
